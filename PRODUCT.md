# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single page, no build step. Bilingual ES/EN with an in-page language toggle (user choice).

## Users

Primary: owners of high-end vacation homes in Guanacaste, Costa Rica (Tamarindo and nearby beach towns), many of them North American, who live elsewhere most of the year and want their villa rented, cared for, and profitable without being involved day to day. They arrive evaluating whether to hand over their property.

## Product Purpose

A luxury property-management company's website. It must let an owner absorb, as fast and effortlessly as possible, what the company does, how handing over a home works, and what standard their home will be kept and presented at. Success: a qualified owner requests a property evaluation.

## Positioning

Showcase-led: the company proves its standard by showing a real managed villa presented at full quality rather than describing services in the abstract. (Positioning beyond this is undecided.)

## Operating Context

Owners typically compare managers remotely, often on a phone, often in English. Guest bookings flow through Airbnb and similar platforms; the manager handles listing, pricing, guest communication, cleaning, maintenance, and owner reporting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Company name: **Pleamar** (provisional, invented; user may replace).
- Contact details, pricing/commission, service area boundaries, and team are undecided; placeholders must be marked.
- Showcase home: the user-supplied Airbnb listing 1306713118635156414 is the flagship property imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

From the Airbnb listing (real): "Luxury ocean view, infinity lap pool, walk to beach" villa in Tamarindo, Guanacaste; 4 bedrooms, 6 beds, 4 baths, sleeps 12; ★5.0 from 36 reviews; rooftop, lap pool, gourmet kitchen, indoor/outdoor dining, BBQ area, daily cleaning included, coded secure access, short walk to downtown Tamarindo, Pacific ocean & sunset views. 97 listing photos (living, kitchen, dining, 4 bedrooms, 4 baths, balcony, pool, rooftop, exterior).

Absent and must not be fabricated as fact: occupancy rates, revenue figures, owner testimonials, number of managed homes, years in business, awards, press. Any illustrative figures must be labeled as examples.

## Product Principles

1. Absorbable in seconds: every section answers one owner question, in plain words.
2. Show the standard, don't claim it: the villa is the proof.
3. Calm confidence over hype; luxury expressed through restraint and precision.
4. Owner peace of mind first: transparency about process and reporting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigable, reduced-motion respected, fully usable at phone width.
