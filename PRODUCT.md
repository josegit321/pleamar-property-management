# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. The bilingual ES/EN master-brand site links to a dedicated Private Retreats page.

## Users

Primary: high-net-worth villa owners and luxury residential developers in Guanacaste who need one accountable team from concept or construction through long-term private operation. Secondary: qualified principals, family offices and strategic partners seeking long-term relationships with the company; and affluent guests or retreat hosts who value privacy, discretion and complete service over discounts.

## Product Purpose

A luxury residential platform website. It must make ORBAYIS's integrated model immediately clear: develop the residence, represent the owner and operate the private experience. Success is a qualified owner, developer or strategic partner requesting a private conversation; retreat guests follow a separate curated path.

## Positioning

**Luxury Residential Developer · Owner’s Representative · Private Villa Operator.** Showcase-led: Casa Caponera proves authorship and accountability from property selection and design through construction, operation and five-star hospitality. ORBAYIS is positioned above conventional property management.

## Operating Context

Owners and developers often evaluate partners remotely, on a phone and in English. They value discretion, continuity, asset protection and a single accountable relationship. The website must filter for fit rather than maximize lead volume. Private capital language must remain non-solicitous and subject to eligibility, due diligence and legal documentation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Company name: **ORBAYIS** (user-provided brand).
- Contact details, operating fees, service-area boundaries, team biographies and form delivery endpoint are undecided; placeholders must be marked and no form may imply real delivery until connected.
- Showcase home: Casa Caponera and its public Airbnb listing are the flagship evidence and imagery.
- The ORBAYIS Private Collection admits both the principal and property selectively; membership is not sold separately.
- The ORBAYIS Private Circle is by referral or private application. Meeting criteria does not guarantee admission.

## Evidence on Hand

From the Airbnb listing (real): "Luxury ocean view, infinity lap pool, walk to beach" villa in Tamarindo, Guanacaste; 4 bedrooms, 6 beds, 4 baths, sleeps 12; ★5.0 from 36 reviews; rooftop, lap pool, gourmet kitchen, indoor/outdoor dining, BBQ area, daily cleaning included, coded secure access, short walk to downtown Tamarindo, Pacific ocean & sunset views. 97 listing photos (living, kitchen, dining, 4 bedrooms, 4 baths, balcony, pool, rooftop, exterior).

From the founder (to be developed through a future interview): he personally selected and sold the property, then designed the complete Casa Caponera concept including hardscape, landscaping, interiors, atmosphere and feeling. The full story belongs on a dedicated Casa Caponera page and must connect each design decision to a lived or operational outcome. See `CASA_CAPONERA_STORY_BRIEF.md`.

Absent and must not be fabricated as fact: occupancy rates, revenue figures, owner testimonials, number of managed homes, years in business, awards, press. Any illustrative figures must be labeled as examples.

## Product Principles

1. Absorbable in seconds: every section answers one qualified visitor question in plain language.
2. Show the standard, don't claim it: Casa Caponera's authorship, imagery and verified reviews are the proof.
3. One vision, one accountable relationship: avoid presenting seven disconnected services.
4. Calm confidence over hype; luxury is expressed through restraint, precision and discretion.
5. Selectivity is mutual: the owner, developer, relationship and property must fit.
6. Never fabricate financial performance, scarcity, testimonials or investment outcomes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigable, reduced-motion respected, fully usable at phone width.
